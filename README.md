# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive, end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance, product category distributions, outlet metrics, and customer preferences for **Blinkit** (India's Last Minute App).

![Blinkit Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/appcreatorabhay/Blinkit-dashboard/raw/main/image_5e443d.png)

---

## 📌 Business Overview

The objective of this project is to conduct a granular analysis of Blinkit's retail data to evaluate key business drivers, customer purchasing patterns, and channel efficiency. By visualizing key performance indicators (KPIs) and operational metrics, this dashboard provides actionable insights to help optimize store inventory, improve product visibility, and boost overall sales revenue[cite: 1].

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Sales:** $19.99K[cite: 1]
* **Average Sales:** $127[cite: 1]
* **Number of Items Sold:** 158[cite: 1]
* **Average Customer Rating:** 4.0 / 5.0[cite: 1]

---

## 🔍 Visualizations & Business Insights

* **Outlet Establishment Growth:** Tracks multi-year sales progression from 2010 to 2020, revealing long-term revenue growth and establishment trends[cite: 1].
* **Fat Content Revenue Breakdown:** Doughnut chart analyzing revenue generation between **Low Fat** ($9.06K) and **Regular** ($10.93K) products[cite: 1].
* **Sales by Outlet Location & Size:** Granular segmentation based on store tiers (e.g., Tier 3) and store footprints (e.g., Medium outlets)[cite: 1].
* **Comprehensive Outlet Matrix:** Detailed cross-analysis evaluating **Grocery Stores**, **Supermarket Type2**, and **Supermarket Type3** across:
  * Total Sales & Item Volume[cite: 1]
  * Average Transaction Value[cite: 1]
  * Average Ratings & Item Visibility Scores[cite: 1]

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills

* **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop[cite: 1]
